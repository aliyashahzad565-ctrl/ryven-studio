RYVEN STUDIOS — CINEMATIC VIDEO-REFERENCE FRONTEND

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the complete ZIP folder.
2. Open the extracted “RYVEN-Studios-Cinematic” folder.
3. Double-click index.html.
4. Use the top navigation to open Home, About, Services, Portfolio, and Contact.
5. Use “View Our Cinematics” on the Home cinematic section to open the
   dedicated scroll-controlled cylinder page. It is intentionally not listed
   in the header navigation.

No localhost, Node.js, npm, installation, or internet connection is required.
